# MRD — Market Requirements Document
Version 1.0 · Owner ChainCraft Studio · Status: Planning hypotheses, not validated market research.

## Market context
Indian small business owners increasingly rely on WhatsApp, websites and social channels. ChainCraft Studio offers websites, e-commerce and redesign services, and wants a disciplined way to connect with **genuinely interested** business operators rather than collecting massive low-quality lists.

## Target customer segments
1. Local established service businesses with outdated or broken websites: clinics, construction, interior/renovation, HVAC, real estate, hospitality, professional services.
2. E-commerce prospects needing catalog, checkout and WhatsApp enquiries (e.g. jewellery and retail).
3. **Inbound warm prospects** from Meta Ads, IndiaMART, own site, referrals and manually supplied lists.
4. Owner-managed businesses seeking an affordable but premium digital presence.

## Buyer problems and observable buying signals
| Problem | Evidence to seek | Safe response |
|---|---|---|
| No functional website | Confirmed from business-owned website/direct discussion | Offer consultation/preview where appropriate |
| Poor mobile UX/performance | Public, repeatable audit with date/device, no speculative guarantees | Explain observed issue and possible impact |
| Need e-commerce | Explicit enquiry, inventory/order needs | Ask checkout/catalog requirements |
| Slow responses / missed enquiries | Prospect reports problem | Offer lead capture/WhatsApp integration |
| High perceived agency risk | Asks for portfolio, timeline, proof | Share verifiable work, clear scope |

A weak website **alone** is not proof a business wants solicitation. Do not infer owner identity or private contact details.

## Alternatives and competition
- Independent website designers and local agencies
- DIY site builders and ecommerce platforms
- WordPress/WooCommerce service agencies
- Lead tools: spreadsheets, general CRMs, marketing automation suites

**Differentiation hypothesis:** hyper-relevant evidence-backed outreach, fast response to warm leads, clear previews, and human-quality website consultation. Test this rather than assert it.

## Discovery channels and acquisition priority
1. Owned inbound leads (highest-confidence and lowest-compliance risk).
2. Existing client referrals and opted-in contacts.
3. Public business websites / explicitly published contact forms only where outreach permitted.
4. Licensed data feeds with contractual permission.
5. LinkedIn **human research only**; no automated extraction or DMs.

## Outcome metrics (initial targets to validate after baseline)
- Qualified enquiry rate; meeting held rate; proposals; deals won.
- Median first-response time for inbound enquiries; appointment show rate.
- Email positive reply rate; opt-out and complaint rates.
- Cost per qualified opportunity; net revenue attributed to channel.

Targets are placeholders until first 30 days of baseline data. Never report invented conversion rates.

## Market validation experiments
- 10 warm inbound leads processed via structured CRM vs current manual process.
- 20 permitted business audits scored by a reviewer for usefulness.
- 20 consented/duly permitted personalized email drafts reviewed for factual claims.
- 5 appointments booked using calendar link/availability.

## Market risks
Unsolicited outreach, false assumptions, poor AI personalisation, LinkedIn access restrictions, Gmail domain reputation, vendor quotas, inaccurate business information, privacy law exposure.

## Success definition
Within a pilot, the owner can trace each qualified opportunity from source → verifiable evidence → approval → conversation → meeting → deal and can stop all outbound activity instantly.

## Optional market hypothesis — AI voice receptionist (deferred)
Some small-business leads may prefer phone discussions in Hindi/Hinglish, but the benefit for ChainCraft is unvalidated. Test whether an explicitly disclosed, opt-in browser-based voice demo improves qualification relative to existing WhatsApp/email workflows. Do not assume prospect preferences, phone reachability, telecom eligibility or conversion uplift without measuring them. This is not a prerequisite for the core opportunity engine.
